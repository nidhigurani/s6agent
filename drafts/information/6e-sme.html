Terms and Conditions

Under the Corporate Flyer Program ("Program"), an eligible corporate customer ("Corporate") of InterGlobe Aviation Limited ("IndiGo") can use the self-booking tool to avail certain benefits on domestic routes, for its registered employees or authorized personnel ("Eligible Travelers") only.

  1. In order for a Corporate Customer to become eligible under this Program, the Corporate must register itself under the Program by completing the registration form and submitting all documents on https://www.goindigo.in/content/indigo/sme/en/register.html.
  2. IndiGo shall, in its sole discretion have the right to approve or reject a registration request of the Corporate. If approved, the same shall be communicated to the Corporate within forty eight (48) working hours of receipt of the request, subject to the form and documents being complete in all respects.
  3. Upon approval of the registration request, Corporate shall be issued a unique Login ID.
  4. Only confirmed bookings made through the Website using the unique Login ID ("Booking") will be eligible for the following benefits:
    • Standard seats are available at up to 30% discounted price on applicable market rates.
    • Ready to eat/non-perishable snack combo including all other snack combos will be available at the applicable market rates.III.
    • Change Fee
      • Domestic Travel : For travel within India, a Change Fee of INR 299 will be charged if change is made to a Booking at least 3 hours before the scheduled departure time. For clarity, no-show charges will be levied if change to a Booking is made within 3 hours prior to the scheduled departure time.
      • International Travel : For travel to/from an international destination a Change Fee of INR 1049 will be charged if change is made to a Booking at least 4 hours before the scheduled departure time. For clarity, no-show charges will be levied if change to a Booking is made within 4 hours prior to the scheduled departure time.
    • Cancellation Fee
      • Domestic Travel : For travel within India, cancellation Fee for Corporate bookings shall be 3-24 hr: NIR 799. Beyond 24hr: INR 499 per passenger per Flight Segment will be levied, in case cancellation is made, at least 3 hours prior to the scheduled departure time of the Booking. No-show charges will be levied if Booking cancellation is made, less than 3 hours prior to the scheduled departure time.
      • International Travel : For travel to/from an international destination, cancellation Fee for Corporate bookings shall be 4-24 hrs: INR 4999. Beyond 24 hrs: INR 2,999 per passenger per flight segment will be levied, in case cancellation is made, at least 4 hours prior to the scheduled departure time of the Booking. No-show charges will be levied if Booking cancellation is made, less than 4 hours prior to the scheduled departure time.
